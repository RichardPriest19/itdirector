Master Data Management (MDM)

Establishing a single, trusted version of core business entities across all systems

Overview

Master Data Management (MDM) establishes a single, authoritative version of core business entities — customers, products, vendors, employees — that is consistently referenced across all systems, eliminating the conflicting, duplicate records that accumulate when each application maintains its own copy. In 2026, MDM platforms increasingly use AI-assisted entity matching and deduplication, and integrate closely with data governance and data mesh initiatives as the trusted source domain teams build products from.

Key Value: MDM eliminates the costly confusion of conflicting customer or product records scattered across CRM, ERP, and billing systems, replacing it with one trusted, continuously synchronized version that every downstream system and analytics effort can rely on.

Core Components

Core Domains

  • Customer/party master data
  • Product & item master data
  • Vendor, employee & location master data

Matching & Quality

  • AI-assisted entity matching & deduplication
  • Data quality rules & validation at point of entry
  • Golden record creation & survivorship rules

Integration & Governance

  • Master data synchronization across downstream systems
  • Data steward ownership of master data domains
  • Alignment with broader data governance & data mesh programs

Best Practices

  • Prioritize the master data domains with the highest cross-system inconsistency first
  • Use AI-assisted matching to scale deduplication beyond manual review capacity
  • Establish clear survivorship rules for conflicting source records
  • Assign dedicated data stewards accountable for each master data domain
  • Synchronize master data changes to downstream systems in near real time

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor MDM program scope, priorities & domain ownership
  • Ensure data steward accountability for each master data domain
  • Fund AI-assisted matching & deduplication tooling
  • Align MDM with broader data governance & data mesh initiatives
