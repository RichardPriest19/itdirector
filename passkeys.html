Passkeys / FIDO2 Authentication

Phishing-resistant, passwordless credentials based on public-key cryptography

Overview

Passkeys are phishing-resistant login credentials built on the FIDO2/WebAuthn standard, replacing passwords with public-key cryptography bound to a device or platform authenticator. In 2026, passkeys are broadly supported across major platforms (Apple, Google, Microsoft), synced across devices via cloud keychains, adopted by enterprises as the default employee authentication method, and mandated in some regulated sectors as part of phishing-resistant MFA requirements.

Key Value: Passkeys eliminate password-based attack vectors — phishing, credential stuffing, and reuse — while improving user experience through fast biometric or device-based sign-in, reducing help-desk password-reset burden.
