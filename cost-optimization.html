Cost Optimization

Systematically reducing IT spend without compromising performance, security, or reliability

Overview

Cost optimization is the systematic practice of reducing IT and cloud spend while preserving necessary performance, security, and reliability — distinguishing genuine waste elimination from cuts that create hidden risk. In 2026, cost optimization practice increasingly uses AI-assisted rightsizing recommendations and automated idle-resource detection, applied across cloud infrastructure, SaaS licensing, and — increasingly — AI/LLM API consumption.

Key Value: Disciplined cost optimization treats spend reduction as an ongoing engineering practice rather than a periodic crisis response, catching waste continuously through automated detection rather than through infrequent, disruptive cost-cutting exercises.

Core Components

Infrastructure Optimization

  • Rightsizing compute & storage resources
  • Idle & orphaned resource detection
  • Reserved/committed-use & spot instance strategy

Licensing & SaaS

  • SaaS license utilization auditing
  • Software asset management alignment
  • Vendor contract renegotiation based on usage data

Emerging Cost Categories

  • AI/LLM API consumption cost management
  • Data egress & transfer cost optimization
  • Observability & logging tool cost management

Best Practices

  • Automate idle & orphaned resource detection rather than relying on periodic manual audits
  • Right-size based on actual utilization data, not initial provisioning estimates
  • Audit SaaS license utilization regularly and eliminate unused seats
  • Extend cost optimization discipline to AI/LLM API consumption
  • Treat cost optimization as continuous engineering practice, not an annual event

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor ongoing cost optimization practice across infrastructure & SaaS
  • Ensure rightsizing & idle-resource automation tooling is in place
  • Track AI/LLM consumption costs as a distinct optimization category
  • Report cost optimization savings & trends to executive leadership
