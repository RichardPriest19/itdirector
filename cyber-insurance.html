Cyber Insurance

Risk transfer coverage for the financial impact of cyber incidents and data breaches

Overview

Cyber insurance provides financial risk transfer for costs associated with cyber incidents — breach response, business interruption, ransomware payments, regulatory fines, and third-party liability. In 2026, insurers require increasingly rigorous security control evidence (MFA, EDR, backup immutability) before binding coverage, and coverage terms distinguish sharply between well-governed and poorly governed security programs in both premium cost and claims outcomes.

Key Value: Cyber insurance doesn't replace security controls — it transfers residual financial risk after controls are in place — and increasingly the underwriting process itself functions as an external validation of whether an organization's security program meets baseline industry expectations.

Core Components

Coverage Types

  • First-party costs (incident response, business interruption)
  • Third-party liability (regulatory fines, customer claims)
  • Ransomware-specific coverage & negotiation support

Underwriting Requirements

  • MFA & privileged access control evidence
  • EDR/XDR deployment verification
  • Immutable backup & disaster recovery validation

Claims & Risk Management

  • Incident response retainer coordination with insurer panels
  • Claims process & documentation requirements
  • Policy renewal risk assessment cycle

Best Practices

  • Maintain the security controls insurers require well before renewal season
  • Document security posture thoroughly to support underwriting & claims
  • Coordinate incident response retainers with insurer-approved panels in advance
  • Review coverage limits & exclusions against realistic incident cost scenarios
  • Treat underwriting requirements as a useful external security baseline check

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Own cyber insurance procurement & renewal relationship
  • Ensure security controls meet current underwriting requirements
  • Coordinate incident response planning with insurer panel requirements
  • Report coverage adequacy & gaps to executive leadership & the board
