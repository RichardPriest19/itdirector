CompTIA A+

The industry-standard entry-level certification for IT support and technical foundations

Overview

CompTIA A+ is the industry-standard, vendor-neutral entry-level certification validating foundational IT support skills — hardware, operating systems, troubleshooting, networking basics, and security fundamentals — commonly required or preferred for help desk and desktop support roles. In 2026, A+ remains the most widely recognized starting credential for IT careers, with its two-exam structure covering both hardware/troubleshooting and operating system/security domains.

Key Value: A+ certification gives hiring managers a reliable baseline signal for entry-level IT support competency, and gives newcomers to IT a structured, comprehensive introduction spanning hardware, OS, networking, and security fundamentals before specializing into any particular track.

Core Components

Exam Structure

  • Core 1 — hardware, networking & troubleshooting
  • Core 2 — operating systems, security & operational procedures
  • Hands-on troubleshooting scenario coverage

Target Audience

  • Entry-level help desk & desktop support staff
  • Career changers entering IT for the first time
  • Foundation before pursuing specialized certification tracks

Certification Value

  • Widely recognized entry-level IT credential
  • Common hiring requirement/preference for support roles
  • Foundation for Network+, Security+ & further specialization

Best Practices

  • Use A+ as the standard entry credential for help desk & desktop support hiring
  • Encourage career changers entering IT to start with A+ as a broad foundation
  • Pair with hands-on troubleshooting practice, not just exam memorization
  • Treat as a foundation for progression toward Network+, Security+ & specialization
  • Recognize A+ holders as ready for structured on-the-job mentorship, not full autonomy

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor CompTIA A+ as a standard entry-level IT certification requirement
  • Use it as a baseline hiring & onboarding credential for support roles
  • Pair with mentorship & hands-on experience for genuine skill development
  • Support career-changer staff pursuing A+ as an entry point into IT
