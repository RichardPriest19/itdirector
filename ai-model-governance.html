AI Model Governance & Model Cards

Documentation, approval, and lifecycle standards for AI models deployed in production

Overview

AI Model Governance establishes documentation, approval, and lifecycle management standards for AI models deployed in production — using tools like model cards (standardized documentation of a model's intended use, performance characteristics, and limitations) to ensure models are evaluated, approved, and monitored consistently. In 2026, model governance increasingly covers third-party and open-weight models alongside internally developed ones, given how much enterprise AI now builds on externally sourced foundation models.

Key Value: Model cards and formal governance close the gap between a data science team's internal knowledge of a model's limitations and what the rest of the organization actually knows when deciding to deploy that model for a new use case — preventing models from being applied well outside the boundaries they were validated for.

Core Components

Documentation Standards

  • Model cards — intended use, performance, limitations, training data summary
  • Version control & change documentation for model updates
  • Third-party & open-weight model documentation review

Approval & Lifecycle

  • Pre-deployment model review & approval gates
  • Ongoing performance monitoring & drift detection
  • Model retirement & deprecation procedures

Risk Management

  • Model risk tiering by decision impact & stakes
  • Bias & fairness validation as part of approval
  • Incident tracking for model failures & unexpected behavior

Best Practices

  • Require model cards for every model before production deployment
  • Tier model risk by decision impact and apply proportionate governance rigor
  • Monitor deployed models continuously for performance drift, not just at launch
  • Extend documentation review to third-party & open-weight models, not just internal ones
  • Maintain a clear model retirement process rather than letting deprecated models linger

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Establish enterprise AI model governance standards & model card requirements
  • Ensure pre-deployment approval gates apply to all production models
  • Fund ongoing model performance & drift monitoring capability
  • Track model risk tiers to focus governance rigor where it matters most
