Attack Surface Management (ASM)

Continuously discovering and monitoring an organization's external-facing digital footprint

Overview

Attack Surface Management (ASM) continuously discovers and monitors an organization's external-facing digital footprint — domains, subdomains, IP ranges, cloud assets, and exposed services — from an outside-in, attacker's-eye perspective, catching unknown or forgotten assets that internal asset inventories routinely miss. In 2026, ASM is increasingly used to catch shadow IT, forgotten subsidiary/M&A infrastructure, and misconfigured cloud assets before attackers find them through the same reconnaissance techniques ASM tools automate.

Key Value: ASM catches the assets internal inventory processes structurally miss — the forgotten subdomain, the shadow IT cloud instance, the M&A-inherited infrastructure nobody documented — by continuously scanning from the same outside-in vantage point an attacker uses during reconnaissance.

Core Components

Discovery Scope

  • Domain, subdomain & DNS record enumeration
  • Exposed cloud asset & service discovery
  • M&A & subsidiary infrastructure inheritance mapping

Risk Assessment

  • Exposed service & vulnerability correlation
  • Shadow IT & unauthorized asset identification
  • Continuous re-scanning for newly exposed assets

Program Integration

  • Feed into vulnerability management prioritization
  • Integration with CTEM validation cycles
  • M&A due diligence & integration risk assessment

Best Practices

  • Run continuous, automated external scanning rather than periodic manual reconnaissance
  • Prioritize newly discovered assets for immediate risk assessment
  • Use ASM specifically during M&A due diligence to surface inherited exposure
  • Feed ASM findings directly into vulnerability management prioritization
  • Treat unexpectedly discovered assets as a signal to investigate governance gaps, not just patch findings

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor ASM tooling deployment for continuous external attack surface visibility
  • Ensure newly discovered assets trigger prompt risk assessment & ownership assignment
  • Use ASM findings during M&A due diligence & integration planning
  • Integrate ASM into the broader CTEM & vulnerability management program
