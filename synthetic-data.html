Synthetic Data

Artificially generated data that mirrors the statistical properties of real datasets

Overview

Synthetic data is artificially generated information — tabular, text, image, or time-series — created by generative models or simulations to statistically resemble real-world data without exposing actual records. In 2026, synthetic data is widely used to train and evaluate AI/ML models where real data is scarce, sensitive, or imbalanced, to support privacy-preserving analytics and software testing, and to augment datasets for edge cases in domains like autonomous systems, healthcare, and financial fraud detection.

Key Value: Synthetic data enables safe, scalable model training and testing while reducing privacy risk, addressing data scarcity, and enabling coverage of rare or sensitive scenarios that would be difficult or unethical to collect from real users.

Core Components (2026)

Generation Techniques

  • Simulation-based synthetic data (physics, agent-based)
  • Statistical resampling & differential privacy techniques

  • Privacy-preserving analytics & test environments
  • Software & QA test data generation

Quality & Validation

  • Privacy leakage & re-identification risk testing
  • Bias detection in generated datasets
