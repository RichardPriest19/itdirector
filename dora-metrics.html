DORA Metrics

The four key metrics research has linked to elite software delivery performance

Overview

DORA metrics — deployment frequency, lead time for changes, mean time to recovery (MTTR), and change failure rate — are the four measures identified by the DevOps Research and Assessment program as most strongly correlated with elite software delivery performance. In 2026, DORA metrics remain the standard baseline for measuring engineering delivery performance, often extended with a fifth metric (reliability) and increasingly tracked automatically through CI/CD and incident management tool integration rather than manual reporting.

Key Value: DORA metrics give engineering leadership an evidence-based, externally validated way to measure delivery performance improvement over time, replacing subjective impressions of team velocity with four concrete, comparable numbers.

Core Components

The Four Metrics

  • Deployment frequency — how often code ships to production
  • Lead time for changes — commit to production duration
  • Mean time to recovery (MTTR) — incident resolution speed
  • Change failure rate — percentage of deployments causing incidents

Measurement Practice

  • Automated capture from CI/CD & incident management tools
  • Team-level vs. organization-level benchmarking
  • Trend tracking over time rather than point-in-time snapshots

Application

  • Elite/high/medium/low performance tier benchmarking
  • Correlating DORA trends with platform & process investments
  • Balancing velocity metrics against reliability (fifth metric)

Best Practices

  • Automate DORA metric capture rather than relying on manual self-reporting
  • Track trends over time rather than treating any single measurement as definitive
  • Benchmark against the established elite/high/medium/low performance tiers
  • Balance deployment frequency & lead time against change failure rate and MTTR
  • Use DORA trends to justify platform & process investment, not just to rank teams

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor DORA metrics tracking across engineering teams
  • Ensure metrics are automated & trend-tracked, not manually reported
  • Use DORA data to inform platform & process investment decisions
  • Avoid using DORA metrics punitively to rank or compare individual teams unfairly
