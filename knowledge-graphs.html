Knowledge Graphs

Structured representations of entities and relationships that ground AI systems in verified facts

Overview

A knowledge graph is a structured representation of entities and the relationships between them, used to organize enterprise data in a way that's both human-interpretable and machine-queryable. In 2026, knowledge graphs have gained renewed enterprise relevance as a grounding mechanism for AI systems — combining with retrieval-augmented generation to reduce hallucination by anchoring LLM outputs to verified, structured facts rather than relying solely on the model's parametric knowledge.

Key Value: Knowledge graphs give AI systems a structured, verifiable source of truth to ground their outputs in — directly addressing the hallucination problem by letting an LLM retrieve and cite specific, traceable facts and relationships rather than generating plausible-sounding but unverified claims.

Core Components

Core Structure

  • Entities, relationships & properties (triples)
  • Ontology & schema design for domain modeling
  • Graph database storage & query (SPARQL, Cypher)

AI Integration

  • Knowledge-graph-grounded retrieval-augmented generation (GraphRAG)
  • Fact verification & hallucination reduction for LLM outputs
  • Structured reasoning over enterprise entity relationships

Enterprise Applications

  • Master data & entity relationship modeling
  • Semantic search & enterprise knowledge discovery
  • Compliance & lineage relationship mapping

Best Practices

  • Design ontology & schema deliberately around actual business questions, not generic modeling
  • Combine knowledge graphs with RAG to ground LLM outputs in verifiable facts
  • Maintain data quality in the graph — a knowledge graph inherits the accuracy of its inputs
  • Use knowledge graphs for genuinely relational questions where they add value over plain search
  • Align knowledge graph entities with master data management for a single source of truth

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Evaluate knowledge graph adoption for AI grounding & enterprise knowledge use cases
  • Ensure knowledge graph data quality is actively maintained
  • Align knowledge graph entities with master data management initiatives
  • Fund GraphRAG integration where hallucination reduction is a priority
