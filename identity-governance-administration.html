Identity Governance & Administration (IGA)

Managing who has access to what, why, and whether that access is still appropriate

Overview

Identity Governance & Administration (IGA) manages the lifecycle of user access — provisioning, access certification/recertification, role-based access control, and deprovisioning — ensuring that who has access to what remains appropriate and auditable over time, distinct from the day-to-day authentication handled by IAM. In 2026, IGA increasingly extends beyond human employees to govern non-human identities (service accounts, API keys, AI agents) as those proliferate and often carry excessive standing privilege.

Key Value: IGA answers the governance question IAM alone doesn't — not just whether someone can authenticate, but whether their accumulated access still makes sense given their current role, and whether that access is being reviewed and revoked on a defensible schedule.

Core Components

Access Lifecycle

  • Automated provisioning & deprovisioning tied to HR events
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) & entitlement catalogs
  • Access certification & recertification campaigns

Governance & Compliance

  • Segregation of duties (SoD) policy enforcement
  • Audit trail & compliance reporting for access reviews
  • Non-human identity (service accounts, API keys) governance

Risk Reduction

  • Excessive/stale access identification & cleanup
  • Privileged access review cadence
  • Orphaned account detection following offboarding

Best Practices

  • Automate provisioning & deprovisioning tightly with HR lifecycle events
  • Run access certification campaigns on a defined, auditable schedule
  • Extend governance explicitly to non-human identities & service accounts
  • Enforce segregation of duties policies for financially or operationally sensitive roles
  • Investigate stale or excessive access patterns proactively, not just at audit time

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Own the enterprise IGA program & access certification cadence
  • Ensure non-human identity governance is included in program scope
  • Enforce segregation of duties for sensitive financial & operational roles
  • Report access governance compliance & findings to auditors & leadership
