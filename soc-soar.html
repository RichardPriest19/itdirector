SOC & SOAR

Security Operations Centers and automated orchestration platforms that scale incident response

Overview

A Security Operations Center (SOC) is the team and processes responsible for continuous security monitoring and incident response, while Security Orchestration, Automation and Response (SOAR) platforms automate repetitive investigation and response steps through playbooks. In 2026, SOC operations increasingly rely on AI-assisted triage to handle rising alert volumes, with SOAR playbooks automating containment actions for well-understood alert types so human analysts focus on genuinely novel investigations.

Key Value: SOAR automation lets a SOC handle rising alert volumes without proportionally scaling headcount, by automating the repetitive triage and containment steps for known alert patterns and freeing analysts to focus on the incidents that need human judgment.

Core Components

SOC Structure

  • Tiered analyst model (Tier 1/2/3) & escalation paths
  • 24/7 coverage models (in-house, MSSP, hybrid)
  • Incident response integration with the SOC

SOAR Capabilities

  • Automated playbooks for common alert types
  • Case management & investigation workflow
  • Integration across SIEM, EDR, ticketing & threat intel tools

Metrics & Maturity

  • Mean time to detect (MTTD) & respond (MTTR)
  • Alert volume vs. analyst capacity tracking
  • Playbook coverage of common alert categories

Best Practices

  • Automate the well-understood, repetitive portion of alert triage first
  • Track MTTD/MTTR to measure SOC effectiveness over time
  • Continuously expand SOAR playbook coverage as new alert patterns stabilize
  • Design escalation paths that get genuinely novel incidents to senior analysts quickly
  • Regularly review automated playbooks to prevent stale or overly broad automation

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor SOC staffing model & coverage strategy (in-house/MSSP/hybrid)
  • Fund SOAR platform investment & playbook development
  • Track SOC performance metrics (MTTD, MTTR, alert volume)
  • Ensure SOC & SOAR integrate with the broader incident response program
