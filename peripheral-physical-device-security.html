Peripheral & Physical Device Security

Protecting cameras, microphones, and other peripherals from tampering during physical access

Overview

Peripheral and physical device security covers protecting cameras, microphones, conferencing hardware, and other sensing peripherals from tampering that could occur during unauthorized or unmonitored physical access to a facility or device. In 2026, this includes physical access controls to sensitive spaces, tamper-evident hardware and enclosures, peripheral device integrity monitoring, and policies restricting unauthorized peripheral or device modification.

Key Value: Physical device integrity controls close the gap that no network-based defense can — protecting against tampering, substitution, or corruption of camera and microphone hardware during moments of physical proximity or access.

Core Components

Physical Access Controls

  • Badge/biometric access control for sensitive equipment rooms
  • Visitor & maintenance-personnel escort policies
  • CCTV & access logging at device installation points

Device Integrity

  • Tamper-evident seals & enclosures on sensitive hardware
  • Peripheral firmware integrity verification
  • Scheduled physical inspection of critical devices

Operational Policy

  • Chain-of-custody for hardware entering secure facilities
  • Vendor maintenance access controls & supervision requirements
  • Incident reporting for suspected tampering

Best Practices

  • Restrict physical access to camera/microphone hardware & equipment rooms
  • Use tamper-evident seals on devices in unsupervised locations
  • Schedule periodic physical inspection of sensitive equipment
  • Supervise vendor/maintenance personnel accessing critical devices
  • Establish a clear reporting process for suspected physical tampering

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Define physical security policy for sensitive peripherals & equipment
  • Ensure access control & logging for equipment rooms
  • Coordinate with facilities & security teams on tamper detection
  • Maintain vendor access supervision requirements
