Business Email Compromise (BEC) Defense

Preventing the social-engineering-driven wire fraud attacks that bypass most technical controls

Overview

Business Email Compromise (BEC) is a class of social-engineering attack where an attacker impersonates an executive, vendor, or trusted partner via email to trick an employee into fraudulent wire transfers, payroll redirection, or sensitive data disclosure — attacks that often involve no malware at all, making them invisible to traditional technical security controls. In 2026, BEC defense combines email authentication protocols, financial process controls (out-of-band verification for payment changes), and targeted awareness training for finance and executive-adjacent staff who are the primary targets.

Key Value: Because BEC attacks are pure social engineering with no malicious payload, they slip past antivirus, EDR, and most email security tools designed to catch malware — making process controls (like mandatory callback verification for payment changes) as important as any technical control.

Core Components

Technical Controls

  • Email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) enforcement
  • Display name spoofing & lookalike domain detection
  • External sender warning banners on inbound email

Process Controls

  • Out-of-band verification required for payment/banking detail changes
  • Dual authorization for wire transfers above defined thresholds
  • Vendor payment change verification procedures

Awareness & Response

  • Targeted training for finance & executive-adjacent staff
  • Rapid response procedures for suspected fraudulent transfers
  • Relationships with banks for fraud recall attempts

Best Practices

  • Enforce DMARC & related email authentication to reduce domain spoofing
  • Require out-of-band verification for any payment or banking detail change request
  • Target awareness training specifically at finance & executive assistant roles
  • Establish dual authorization thresholds for wire transfers
  • Maintain bank relationships & procedures for rapid fraud recall attempts

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor BEC-specific awareness training & process controls
  • Ensure payment change verification procedures are mandatory & enforced
  • Enforce email authentication protocols (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) organization-wide
  • Establish rapid response procedures & bank relationships for fraud recall
