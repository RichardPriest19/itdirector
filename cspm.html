Cloud Security Posture Management (CSPM)

Continuously identifying and remediating misconfigurations and compliance drift across cloud environments

Overview

Cloud Security Posture Management (CSPM) continuously scans cloud environments for misconfigurations, compliance drift, and policy violations — the exposed storage bucket, the overly permissive IAM role, the unencrypted database — that remain among the most common root causes of cloud breaches. In 2026, CSPM capability is increasingly bundled into broader Cloud-Native Application Protection Platforms (CNAPP) alongside CWPP runtime protection, though the CSPM function itself — configuration and compliance visibility — remains a distinct and essential capability.

Key Value: CSPM addresses the specific, high-frequency failure mode of cloud breaches — misconfiguration rather than sophisticated exploitation — by continuously comparing actual cloud configuration against security best practice and compliance benchmarks, catching drift before it becomes an exposed asset.

Core Components

Detection Scope

  • Misconfigured storage, network & IAM permissions
  • Compliance benchmark drift (CIS, PCI-DSS, SOC 2)
  • Excessive permission & privilege escalation paths

Operational Practices

  • Continuous, automated scanning across all cloud accounts
  • Risk-based prioritization of findings by exposure & blast radius
  • Auto-remediation for well-understood, low-risk findings

Governance Integration

  • Multi-cloud policy consistency across AWS/Azure/GCP
  • Integration with CWPP for full CNAPP coverage
  • Compliance reporting for audits & regulatory requirements

Best Practices

  • Enable continuous scanning across every cloud account, not periodic point-in-time audits
  • Prioritize findings by actual exposure & blast radius, not raw finding count
  • Auto-remediate well-understood, low-risk misconfigurations to reduce backlog
  • Apply consistent policy baselines across multi-cloud environments
  • Integrate CSPM findings with broader CNAPP & vulnerability management workflows

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Deploy CSPM coverage across all cloud accounts & subscriptions
  • Ensure findings are prioritized & remediated against defined SLAs
  • Maintain consistent security baselines across multi-cloud environments
  • Use CSPM compliance reporting to support audit & regulatory requirements
