Mobile Device & Communication Security

Protecting corporate mobile devices and the messaging channels attackers commonly target

Overview

Mobile device and communication security covers protecting smartphones, tablets, and the SMS/messaging channels they rely on from interception, malicious apps, and unauthorized access. In 2026, enterprise practice centers on Mobile Device Management (MDM) / Unified Endpoint Management (UEM), mobile threat defense (MTD) platforms detecting malicious apps and network attacks, encrypted messaging as the default for sensitive communication, and monitoring for anomalous SMS/carrier-level activity such as SIM-swap attempts.

Key Value: Strong mobile device governance and encrypted-by-default communication channels close off SMS interception, malicious app installation, and SIM-based attack vectors that remain among the most common ways sensitive mobile communications are compromised.

Core Components

Device Management

  • Mobile Device Management (MDM) / Unified Endpoint Management (UEM)
  • App allowlisting & mobile application vetting
  • Remote wipe & compliance enforcement

Threat Detection

  • Mobile Threat Defense (MTD) for malicious app & network detection
  • SIM-swap & carrier-level anomaly monitoring
  • Jailbreak/root detection & compliance blocking

Communication Protection

  • End-to-end encrypted messaging for sensitive communications
  • Migration away from SMS for authentication where possible
  • Carrier & telecom security coordination for high-risk personnel

Best Practices

  • Enforce MDM/UEM enrollment for all corporate-connected mobile devices
  • Deploy mobile threat defense to detect malicious apps & network attacks
  • Prefer app-based or hardware authenticators over SMS for MFA
  • Monitor for SIM-swap indicators on executive & high-risk accounts
  • Standardize on encrypted messaging for sensitive business communication

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Define mobile device management & app governance policy
  • Deploy mobile threat defense across the managed device fleet
  • Reduce reliance on SMS for authentication enterprise-wide
  • Coordinate with carriers on SIM-swap protections for high-risk personnel
