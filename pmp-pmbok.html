PMP & PMBOK

The Project Management Institute's flagship certification and body of knowledge guide

Overview

The Project Management Professional (PMP) certification, administered by the Project Management Institute (PMI), validates project management expertise against the practices described in the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK Guide). In 2026, PMP certification reflects PMBOK's continued evolution toward principles-based guidance covering predictive, agile, and hybrid delivery approaches, rather than the purely process-heavy waterfall orientation of earlier editions.

Key Value: PMP certification remains one of the most widely recognized project management credentials globally, giving IT and business stakeholders a common signal of proven competency across the full range of delivery approaches — predictive, agile, and hybrid — that PMBOK's current edition addresses.

Core Components

PMBOK Framework

  • Project management principles (current PMBOK edition)
  • Performance domains spanning predictive & agile delivery
  • Value delivery system for organizational project management

PMP Certification

  • Experience & education eligibility requirements
  • Exam covering people, process & business environment domains
  • Continuing certification requirements (PDUs) for renewal

Application

  • Cross-industry project & program management roles
  • Common baseline expectation for senior IT project managers
  • Foundation for advanced PMI certifications (PgMP, PfMP)

Best Practices

  • Treat PMBOK as principles-based guidance to adapt, not a rigid process to follow verbatim
  • Pursue PMP for staff managing significant project scope, budget & risk
  • Maintain certification currency through continuing education (PDUs)
  • Apply PMBOK principles across predictive, agile & hybrid delivery approaches as fits the project
  • Use PMP as one signal among several when evaluating project management competency

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor PMP certification for project & program management staff
  • Ensure PDU continuing education requirements are supported & tracked
  • Apply PMBOK principles pragmatically across delivery approaches used in the organization
  • Use certification status appropriately in project manager role requirements
