GreenOps / Sustainable IT

Reducing the environmental footprint of IT infrastructure through carbon-aware operational practice

Overview

GreenOps (or Sustainable IT) applies the same operational discipline that FinOps brought to cost management to an organization's environmental footprint — measuring and reducing the carbon emissions, energy consumption, and e-waste generated by IT infrastructure. In 2026, GreenOps practice is driven by growing regulatory reporting requirements (corporate sustainability disclosure), rising data center energy demand from AI workloads specifically, and carbon-aware workload scheduling that shifts computation to times/locations with cleaner energy availability.

Key Value: GreenOps gives IT organizations a measurable, actionable framework for sustainability — turning often-vague corporate environmental commitments into concrete, trackable practices like carbon-aware scheduling and hardware lifecycle extension that also frequently reduce cost.

Core Components

Measurement

  • Cloud & data center carbon emissions tracking
  • AI workload energy consumption measurement
  • E-waste & hardware lifecycle impact tracking

Operational Practices

  • Carbon-aware workload scheduling & placement
  • Hardware lifecycle extension & responsible disposal/recycling
  • Server & data center utilization optimization to reduce idle waste

Governance & Reporting

  • Corporate sustainability disclosure & regulatory reporting alignment
  • Sustainability KPIs integrated into IT scorecards
  • Vendor sustainability criteria in procurement decisions

Best Practices

  • Measure carbon emissions & energy consumption before setting reduction targets
  • Schedule carbon-flexible workloads for times/regions with cleaner energy where feasible
  • Extend hardware lifecycle deliberately rather than defaulting to frequent refresh cycles
  • Track AI workload energy consumption as a distinct, fast-growing category
  • Incorporate vendor sustainability criteria into procurement & RFP evaluation

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor GreenOps/Sustainable IT measurement & reduction program
  • Ensure sustainability reporting meets current regulatory disclosure requirements
  • Track AI workload energy consumption trends specifically
  • Integrate sustainability criteria into vendor procurement decisions
