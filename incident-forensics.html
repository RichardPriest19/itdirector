Incident Forensics & eDiscovery

Structured investigation, evidence collection, and legal preservation following a security incident

Overview

Incident forensics is the structured investigation of a security incident to determine root cause, scope, and impact, while eDiscovery covers the identification, preservation, and production of electronically stored information for legal or regulatory proceedings. In 2026, this discipline increasingly integrates cloud-native forensic collection (since evidence lives across SaaS and IaaS platforms, not just on-premises disks) with established chain-of-custody and legal hold procedures.

Key Value: Rigorous incident forensics converts a chaotic breach response into a defensible, legally sound investigation — establishing exactly what happened, what data was affected, and preserving evidence in a form that will hold up to regulatory or litigation scrutiny.

Core Components

Investigation Process

  • Scoping & timeline reconstruction of the incident
  • Root cause analysis across affected systems
  • Cloud-native evidence collection (SaaS/IaaS logs)

Legal & Compliance

  • Chain-of-custody documentation for all evidence
  • Legal hold procedures for relevant data
  • Coordination with outside counsel & regulators

Program Readiness

  • Pre-established forensic tooling & playbooks
  • Retainer relationships with external forensic firms
  • Regular tabletop exercises testing forensic readiness

Best Practices

  • Establish forensic readiness — tooling and playbooks — before an incident occurs
  • Maintain rigorous chain-of-custody documentation for all evidence collected
  • Coordinate closely with legal counsel from the earliest stage of an investigation
  • Extend evidence collection capability to cloud/SaaS platforms, not just on-prem systems
  • Test forensic readiness regularly through incident simulation exercises

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Fund forensic readiness capability & external retainer relationships
  • Ensure chain-of-custody & legal hold procedures are documented & tested
  • Coordinate incident forensics closely with legal & compliance functions
  • Report investigation findings & lessons learned to leadership
