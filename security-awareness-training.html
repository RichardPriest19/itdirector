Security Awareness Training

Building a security-conscious workforce through ongoing education and simulated exercises

Overview

Security awareness training educates employees on recognizing and responding to security threats — phishing, social engineering, data handling, and secure behavior — through ongoing education rather than one-time onboarding sessions. In 2026, mature programs use continuous, role-based micro-training, realistic phishing simulations with personalized follow-up, and behavior-change metrics rather than simple completion-rate tracking.

Key Value: Well-designed security awareness training turns employees from the most commonly exploited attack vector into an active detection layer, since phishing and social engineering remain the entry point for the large majority of successful breaches regardless of technical controls.

Core Components

Training Design

  • Role-based, continuous micro-training modules
  • Realistic phishing simulation campaigns
  • Just-in-time training triggered by risky behavior

Culture Building

  • Positive reinforcement for reporting suspicious activity
  • Executive & leadership visible participation
  • Security champions program within business units

Measurement

  • Behavior-change metrics beyond completion rates
  • Phishing simulation click/report rate trends
  • Repeat-offender identification & targeted follow-up

Best Practices

  • Move beyond annual completion-rate training to continuous, role-based education
  • Run realistic phishing simulations with immediate, constructive feedback
  • Measure behavior change, not just training completion percentages
  • Reward and normalize reporting of suspicious activity
  • Target additional training toward repeat higher-risk individuals

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor an ongoing security awareness program & budget
  • Ensure training content stays current with emerging threat patterns
  • Track behavior-change metrics & report trends to leadership
  • Model visible security-conscious behavior from executive leadership
