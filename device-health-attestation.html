Device Health Attestation & Compliance Gating

Verifying a device's security posture before granting it access to enterprise resources

Overview

Device health attestation verifies that a device meets defined security requirements — patch level, disk encryption, endpoint protection status, boot integrity — before granting it access to enterprise resources, and continues to re-verify throughout the session rather than trusting a one-time check. In 2026, attestation is a core building block of practical zero-trust deployments, using TPM-backed hardware attestation, mobile device compliance signals, and continuous conditional-access policy evaluation tied into identity and network access decisions.

Key Value: Device attestation closes the gap between having a device management policy on paper and actually enforcing it at the access-control layer — an unpatched or non-compliant device is blocked from sensitive resources automatically, rather than relying on manual audit.

Core Components

Attestation Signals

  • TPM-backed hardware & boot integrity attestation
  • Patch level, disk encryption & endpoint protection status
  • Mobile device compliance signals (jailbreak/root detection, OS version)

Enforcement

  • Conditional access policies gating resource access on compliance status
  • Continuous re-verification throughout active sessions
  • Automated quarantine/remediation workflows for non-compliant devices

Integration

  • UEM/MDM compliance signal feeds into identity providers
  • Network access control tied to device health status
  • Reporting & audit trails for compliance posture over time

Best Practices

  • Tie conditional access decisions directly to device compliance signals
  • Re-evaluate device health continuously, not just at initial sign-in
  • Automate quarantine & remediation workflows for non-compliant devices
  • Use hardware-backed attestation (TPM) wherever devices support it
  • Extend attestation coverage to mobile & BYOD devices consistently

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Define device compliance requirements & attestation policy
  • Integrate attestation signals with identity & conditional access systems
  • Ensure continuous re-verification is enforced, not one-time checks
  • Track compliance posture trends & remediation effectiveness
