IoT & Smart Device Security

Managing the expanded attack surface introduced by connected devices in enterprise environments

Overview

IoT & smart device security addresses the risks introduced by connected devices — smart TVs, conferencing systems, sensors, cameras, and building automation — that often ship with limited security controls, infrequent firmware updates, and always-on microphones or cameras. In 2026, enterprise practice centers on network segmentation for IoT device classes, device inventory & risk scoring, mandatory firmware update policies, and scrutiny of default data-collection behavior in devices deployed near sensitive conversations or facilities.

Key Value: Treating IoT and smart devices as untrusted network citizens — segmented, inventoried, and monitored — prevents them from becoming a foothold for lateral movement or an unintentional surveillance risk in sensitive spaces.

Core Components

Inventory & Risk Assessment

  • IoT device discovery & asset inventory
  • Vendor security posture & update-cadence review
  • Risk scoring for devices with microphones/cameras in sensitive areas

Network Controls

  • Dedicated IoT VLANs & micro-segmentation
  • Default-deny outbound policy for device classes
  • Network access control (NAC) for device onboarding

Device Hardening

  • Mandatory firmware update enforcement
  • Disabling unused features (voice assistants, always-on mics) where not required
  • Physical placement policy for sensitive meeting spaces

Best Practices

  • Maintain a current inventory of all connected/IoT devices on the network
  • Segment IoT devices onto isolated VLANs with restricted access
  • Disable unnecessary always-on sensing features by default
  • Require vendor firmware updates as a procurement condition
  • Restrict smart devices in rooms used for sensitive discussions

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Establish IoT/smart-device procurement & security standards
  • Ensure network segmentation for all device classes
  • Maintain device inventory & firmware compliance tracking
  • Set policy for sensitive-space device deployment
