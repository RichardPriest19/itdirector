Platform Engineering

Building internal developer platforms that provide self-service, golden-path infrastructure

Overview

Platform engineering builds and operates internal developer platforms (IDPs) — self-service tooling, golden paths, and abstracted infrastructure — that let application teams deploy and operate software without needing deep infrastructure expertise. In 2026, platform engineering is a distinct discipline from traditional DevOps/SRE, with dedicated platform teams treating the IDP itself as a product with its own roadmap and internal customers.

Key Value: Platform engineering reduces cognitive load on application teams by providing paved, self-service paths for common infrastructure needs, letting developers focus on business logic while the platform team owns the underlying complexity and standards.

Core Components

Platform Capabilities

  • Self-service infrastructure provisioning
  • Golden-path templates & scaffolding for new services
  • Centralized developer portal (e.g., Backstage)

Team Structure

  • Dedicated platform team treating the IDP as a product
  • Internal customer feedback loops & roadmap
  • Clear division between platform & application team responsibilities

Standardization

  • Consistent CI/CD, observability & security baked into golden paths
  • Policy-as-code enforcement embedded in self-service workflows
  • Metrics on platform adoption & developer satisfaction

Best Practices

  • Treat the internal developer platform as a product with real internal customers
  • Build golden paths that make the secure, compliant option the easiest option
  • Gather regular feedback from application teams using the platform
  • Measure platform adoption & developer satisfaction, not just uptime
  • Avoid over-abstracting — preserve enough flexibility for genuine edge cases

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor a dedicated platform engineering team & roadmap
  • Ensure golden paths embed security & compliance by default
  • Track platform adoption metrics & developer satisfaction
  • Balance platform standardization against application team flexibility needs
