Software Asset Management (SAM)

Tracking software inventory and usage to ensure licensing compliance and cost efficiency

Overview

Software Asset Management (SAM) tracks the full inventory, usage, and licensing entitlements of software across an organization, ensuring compliance with vendor license terms while identifying cost optimization opportunities from underutilized licenses. In 2026, SAM practice extends to SaaS subscription management alongside traditional on-premises licensing, and increasingly relies on automated discovery tools rather than periodic manual audits.

Key Value: Disciplined SAM practice avoids the two costly failure modes it sits between — expensive licensing compliance penalties from under-licensing, and wasted spend on unused entitlements from over-licensing — by maintaining continuous, accurate visibility into what's actually deployed and used.

Core Components

Inventory & Discovery

  • Automated software discovery across endpoints & servers
  • SaaS subscription & seat utilization tracking
  • License entitlement reconciliation against actual deployment

Compliance Management

  • Vendor audit readiness & true-up preparation
  • License model compliance (per-user, per-core, subscription)
  • Software usage rights & terms tracking

Cost Optimization

  • Underutilized license identification & reclamation
  • License optimization ahead of renewal negotiations
  • Alignment with FinOps for SaaS spend visibility

Best Practices

  • Automate software discovery rather than relying on periodic manual audits
  • Reconcile actual usage against license entitlements regularly, not just before vendor audits
  • Reclaim and reallocate underutilized licenses proactively
  • Extend SAM practice to cover SaaS subscriptions, not just traditional on-prem licensing
  • Use usage data to negotiate renewal terms from a position of informed strength

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Own enterprise software asset management program & tooling
  • Ensure automated discovery provides accurate license compliance visibility
  • Drive cost optimization through underutilized license reclamation
  • Prepare for vendor audits proactively using continuous SAM data
