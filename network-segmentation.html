Network Segmentation

Dividing networks into isolated zones to contain breaches and limit lateral movement

Overview

Network segmentation divides a network into isolated zones — by function, sensitivity, or trust level — so that a compromise in one segment cannot freely spread to others. In 2026, segmentation practice has moved from coarse VLAN-based zoning toward fine-grained micro-segmentation enforced at the workload level, often software-defined and tightly coupled with zero-trust access policy.

Key Value: Effective segmentation is one of the highest-leverage controls against ransomware and lateral movement — it doesn't prevent every initial compromise, but it caps the damage a single foothold can do by design.

Core Components

Segmentation Approaches

  • Traditional VLAN & subnet-based zoning
  • Micro-segmentation at the workload/container level
  • Software-defined segmentation policy enforcement

Design Considerations

  • Segmentation by data sensitivity & business function
  • East-west traffic inspection between segments
  • OT/IT segmentation for industrial environments

Operations

  • Segmentation policy documentation & change control
  • Regular validation testing of segment isolation
  • Monitoring for unauthorized cross-segment traffic

Best Practices

  • Segment by sensitivity & function, not just physical network topology
  • Extend segmentation to the workload level with micro-segmentation where feasible
  • Regularly test that segment isolation actually holds under realistic scenarios
  • Monitor and alert on unexpected east-west traffic between segments
  • Prioritize segmentation of OT/ICS environments from general IT networks

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Define enterprise network segmentation strategy & standards
  • Ensure segmentation policy is enforced & regularly validated
  • Prioritize segmentation of critical & sensitive network zones
  • Monitor for segmentation policy violations & drift
