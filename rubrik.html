Rubrik

A modern, security-led data protection platform focused on cyber resilience and ransomware recovery

Overview

Rubrik is a newer data-protection vendor (founded in 2014) that entered the market with a modern, simple, web-scale approach to backup and has since positioned itself firmly around cyber resilience — data security and recovery from ransomware — rather than backup alone. Its pitch is less "how do we back up your data" and more "how do we get you running again quickly and safely after an attack". This security-first framing, combined with an API-first design and a clean management experience, has made it a prominent challenger to the established players.

Key Capabilities

  • Immutable backups — data written so it cannot be altered or deleted by attackers.
  • Ransomware recovery — rapidly identify a clean recovery point and restore.
  • Anomaly detection — spot unusual change patterns that may indicate an attack.
  • Sensitive data discovery — find and classify sensitive information across the estate.
  • Threat hunting & monitoring — security-oriented analysis of protected data.
  • Broad coverage — data centre, cloud and SaaS workloads via the Rubrik Security Cloud.

Positioning

Rubrik's distinguishing move is to treat backup as a security function. Because backups are both the last line of defence and a frequent target of ransomware, framing data protection as cyber resilience resonates strongly with boards and security teams. The company became publicly listed in 2024 and has a notable partnership with Microsoft. Its relative youth means a modern architecture, balanced against the longer track record of incumbents.

IT Director Considerations

  • Strong fit where ransomware resilience and security are the primary drivers
  • Recovery speed and confidence in a clean restore point as key evaluation criteria
  • The security capabilities (discovery, anomaly detection) as value beyond backup
  • Ease of use and API-first automation for leaner teams
  • Cost relative to established alternatives
  • How it integrates with the wider security stack and DR/BCP strategy

Rubrik vs Veeam, Commvault & Cohesity

Rubrik is the modern, security-led option, strongest where cyber resilience and ransomware recovery lead the decision. Veeam is flexible, software-only and rooted in virtualisation, favoured for ease of use and broad coverage. Commvault is the long-established, comprehensive enterprise platform with deep data-management capabilities. Cohesity centres on consolidating secondary data at web scale and, after combining with Veritas' enterprise data protection business, is now one of the largest players. All four now compete heavily on immutability, anti-ransomware and rapid recovery — so the choice tends to hinge on whether security framing, breadth, flexibility or scale matters most for your estate.
