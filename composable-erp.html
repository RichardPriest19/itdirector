Composable ERP

Assembling modular, best-of-breed business applications instead of monolithic ERP suites

Overview

Composable ERP replaces the traditional monolithic, single-vendor ERP suite with a modular architecture of best-of-breed applications connected through APIs and integration platforms — letting organizations swap individual capabilities (finance, HR, procurement) independently rather than being locked into one vendor's full suite. In 2026, composable ERP is enabled by mature integration platforms (iPaaS), API-first vendor architectures, and growing enterprise appetite to avoid the multi-year, high-risk monolithic ERP replacement projects of the past.

Key Value: Composable ERP reduces the all-or-nothing risk of traditional ERP transformation projects, letting organizations modernize individual business capabilities incrementally and choose genuinely best-fit tools for each function rather than accepting a single vendor's compromises across every module.

Core Components

Architecture Approach

  • API-first modular application selection per business capability
  • Integration Platform as a Service (iPaaS) as the connective layer
  • Composable business capability mapping

Selection Criteria

  • Best-of-breed evaluation per functional domain
  • Integration complexity & data consistency trade-off assessment
  • Vendor API maturity & interoperability evaluation

Governance

  • Master data management for cross-application consistency
  • Integration architecture ownership & standards
  • Incremental migration planning vs. monolithic ERP replacement

Best Practices

  • Map business capabilities clearly before selecting individual composable modules
  • Invest in a robust integration platform as the connective foundation
  • Prioritize master data management to keep data consistent across modular applications
  • Evaluate vendor API maturity as a key selection criterion, not an afterthought
  • Plan incremental capability-by-capability migration rather than a single big-bang cutover

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Define composable ERP strategy & business capability architecture
  • Ensure integration platform investment supports modular application connectivity
  • Prioritize master data management for cross-application consistency
  • Oversee incremental migration planning & vendor selection governance
