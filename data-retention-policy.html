Data Retention Policy

Defining how long data is kept, and ensuring timely, defensible disposal when it's no longer needed

Overview

Data retention policy defines how long different categories of data must be kept to meet business, legal, and regulatory requirements, and establishes defensible disposal processes once retention periods expire. In 2026, retention policy enforcement is increasingly automated within data platforms and SaaS applications, reducing both storage costs and the breach exposure created by indefinitely retained data that serves no ongoing purpose.

Key Value: A disciplined retention policy directly reduces breach exposure and storage cost by ensuring data doesn't accumulate indefinitely past its useful or legally required life — the data an organization no longer holds cannot be the subject of a future breach.

Core Components

Policy Framework

  • Retention schedules by data category & regulatory requirement
  • Legal hold override procedures for litigation/investigation
  • Defensible disposal & destruction certification

Enforcement

  • Automated retention enforcement in data platforms & SaaS apps
  • Retention policy applied consistently across backups & archives
  • Exception handling for data under legal hold

Governance

  • Regular retention schedule review against changing regulations
  • Cross-functional alignment (legal, compliance, IT, records management)
  • Retention compliance auditing & reporting

Best Practices

  • Define retention schedules by data category, not a single blanket policy
  • Automate retention enforcement in data platforms rather than relying on manual cleanup
  • Apply retention consistently across production data, backups & archives
  • Maintain clear legal hold procedures that override standard retention rules
  • Review retention schedules periodically as regulations & business needs change

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Own enterprise data retention policy definition & governance
  • Ensure automated enforcement is implemented across data platforms
  • Coordinate retention policy with legal on litigation hold requirements
  • Audit retention compliance & report gaps to leadership

Resources

  • NIST SP 800-53 records retention & disposal controls
  • ISO 15489 records management standard
  • Sedona Conference guidance on information governance & retention
  • Regulatory retention requirements (GDPR, HIPAA, SOX, industry-specific)
