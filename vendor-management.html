Vendor Management

Managing supplier relationships from onboarding through performance review and renewal

Overview

Vendor management covers the full lifecycle of supplier relationships — onboarding, performance monitoring, relationship governance, and renewal or offboarding decisions — ensuring vendors deliver expected value while risk is actively managed throughout the relationship. In 2026, vendor management increasingly integrates directly with vendor risk assessment and software asset management data, giving IT leadership a unified view of vendor performance, cost, and risk in one place.

Key Value: Proactive vendor management — rather than reactive engagement only at renewal time — catches underperformance and risk early, and gives IT leadership real negotiating leverage informed by continuous performance data rather than last-minute renewal pressure.

Core Components

Lifecycle Management

  • Vendor onboarding & due diligence process
  • Performance monitoring against SLAs & KPIs
  • Renewal, renegotiation & offboarding decisions

Relationship Governance

  • Regular business reviews with key vendors
  • Escalation paths for service issues
  • Vendor scorecard & performance tracking

Integration

  • Alignment with vendor risk assessment data
  • Software asset management & license utilization visibility
  • Contract lifecycle management coordination

Best Practices

  • Monitor vendor performance continuously, not just at renewal time
  • Use vendor scorecards to make renewal & renegotiation decisions data-driven
  • Hold regular business reviews with strategically important vendors
  • Integrate vendor management data with risk assessment & asset management
  • Build clear escalation paths for service issues before they become crises

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Own enterprise vendor management program & governance
  • Ensure vendor performance is tracked systematically against SLAs
  • Coordinate vendor management with risk assessment & procurement
  • Use vendor performance data to drive renewal negotiation strategy
