GSOM: GIAC Security Operations Manager

SANS SOC strategy, team leadership, and detection engineering certification

Overview

GSOM (GIAC Security Operations Manager) is a SANS/GIAC certification focused on the leadership and strategic dimensions of running a Security Operations Center — team structure, detection engineering prioritization, metrics, and executive communication — distinct from the hands-on analyst-level certifications (GCIA, GCIH) that focus on individual technical investigation skills. In 2026, GSOM is commonly pursued by SOC managers and security leaders responsible for the operational effectiveness of detection and response programs, not just individual analyst technique.

Key Value: GSOM validates the specific skill set needed to run a SOC as an effective organization — staffing models, detection engineering prioritization, and metrics that demonstrate value to leadership — complementing the deep technical certifications analysts hold individually.

Core Components

Exam Coverage

  • SOC strategy, staffing models & maturity assessment
  • Detection engineering prioritization & coverage planning
  • SOC metrics & executive/board-level reporting

Target Audience

  • SOC managers & security operations leaders
  • Security leaders overseeing detection & response programs
  • Analysts transitioning into SOC leadership roles

Complementary Certifications

  • GCIA/GCIH for hands-on analyst technical depth
  • GCTI for threat intelligence specialization
  • CISM for broader security management scope

Best Practices

  • Pursue GSOM specifically for SOC leadership & management roles, not analyst technical depth
  • Combine with hands-on analyst certifications on the team for balanced SOC capability
  • Apply GSOM's metrics & reporting frameworks to demonstrate SOC value to leadership
  • Use detection engineering prioritization principles to focus limited SOC resources
  • Maintain certification currency as SOC practices & threats evolve

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor GSOM certification for SOC management & leadership staff
  • Use GSOM-aligned metrics to report SOC effectiveness to executive leadership
  • Ensure SOC leadership has both strategic & technical certification coverage
  • Apply detection engineering prioritization frameworks to SOC resource allocation
