ROI Measurement for IT Investments

Quantifying the business value returned by technology investments to inform prioritization

Overview

ROI measurement for IT investments quantifies the business value — cost savings, revenue growth, risk reduction, productivity gains — returned by a technology investment relative to its total cost, informing prioritization and post-investment accountability. In 2026, ROI measurement increasingly must account for AI initiatives where value often accrues through productivity gains and quality improvements that are harder to quantify than direct cost savings, requiring broader value frameworks than pure cost-based ROI.

Key Value: Disciplined ROI measurement — including honest tracking of whether projected returns actually materialized — turns technology investment decisions from opinion-driven advocacy into an evidence-based prioritization process leadership and finance can trust.

Core Components

Measurement Framework

  • Direct cost savings & revenue impact quantification
  • Productivity & efficiency gain estimation methodology
  • Risk reduction value quantification (avoided cost)

AI & Emerging Tech Considerations

  • Broader value frameworks beyond direct cost ROI for AI initiatives
  • Quality & capability improvement measurement
  • Time-to-value tracking for AI/automation investments

Governance

  • Post-investment review against original ROI projections
  • Portfolio-level ROI reporting for prioritization
  • Standardized ROI methodology across the investment portfolio

Best Practices

  • Use a consistent ROI methodology across the technology investment portfolio
  • Track actual post-investment outcomes against original projections honestly
  • Extend ROI frameworks to capture productivity & quality gains for AI initiatives
  • Include risk-reduction value, not just direct cost savings or revenue impact
  • Report portfolio-level ROI trends to inform future prioritization decisions

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Establish standardized ROI measurement methodology for IT investments
  • Require post-investment review against original ROI projections
  • Extend ROI frameworks appropriately for AI & emerging technology initiatives
  • Use portfolio ROI data to inform investment prioritization decisions
