Cohesity

Web-scale data protection and management that consolidates secondary data onto one platform

Overview

Cohesity (founded in 2013) built its reputation on tackling "mass data fragmentation" — the sprawl of backup, file, object and test data across disconnected silos — by consolidating it onto a single, web-scale platform. Beyond backup, it positions itself around data management, security and AI-driven insight into an organisation's data. In December 2024 Cohesity completed its combination with Veritas' enterprise data protection business, bringing the long-established NetBackup product into its portfolio and making it, by its own account, the world's largest data-protection software provider.

Key Capabilities

  • Consolidated platform — backup, files, objects and more managed together rather than in silos.
  • Scale-out architecture — grows by adding nodes, designed for large environments.
  • Anti-ransomware — immutability, threat detection and rapid recovery.
  • Helios — SaaS-based management across the estate.
  • AI-driven insight — search and analysis over protected data.
  • NetBackup — the enterprise product gained from the Veritas combination, supported alongside Cohesity's own.

The Veritas Combination

The 2024 deal was unusual — backup vendors rarely buy a direct competitor outright. It brought Veritas NetBackup, its appliances and the Veritas Alta SaaS offering into Cohesity, dramatically expanding its enterprise customer base and global footprint. Some former Veritas products not part of the deal (such as Backup Exec and InfoScale) were spun into a separate company, Arctera. Cohesity has committed to continue supporting both its own and the acquired Veritas products, so existing customers can migrate on their own timeline rather than being forced to switch.

IT Director Considerations

  • Strong fit for large environments seeking to consolidate fragmented secondary data
  • For existing NetBackup customers, the change of ownership and the long-term product roadmap
  • Integration maturity, given how recently the two organisations combined
  • Cyber-resilience features (immutability, anomaly detection) as ransomware defences
  • Scale and total cost of ownership for a platform aimed at the upper end of the market
  • How it fits the wider disaster-recovery and business-continuity strategy

Cohesity vs Veeam, Commvault & Rubrik

Cohesity emphasises consolidating secondary data at web scale and, post-Veritas, brings enormous reach and the established NetBackup product. Veeam is flexible, software-only and rooted in virtualisation, favoured for ease of use. Commvault is the long-established, comprehensive enterprise platform with deep data-management capabilities. Rubrik is a newer, security-led entrant focused on cyber resilience and fast ransomware recovery. The four increasingly compete on the same priorities — immutability, anti-ransomware and rapid recovery — so the decision usually comes down to estate scale, consolidation goals, and existing vendor relationships.
