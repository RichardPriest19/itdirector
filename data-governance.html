Data Governance

The framework of policies, roles, and standards that ensure data is accurate, secure, and well-managed

Overview

Data governance establishes the policies, roles, standards, and processes that ensure data across an organization is accurate, consistent, secure, and used appropriately throughout its lifecycle. In 2026, data governance programs increasingly must account for AI training data provenance, cross-border data transfer restrictions, and automated policy enforcement embedded directly into data platforms rather than relying solely on manual review processes.

Key Value: Strong data governance turns data from a scattered, inconsistently trusted liability into a well-understood, appropriately protected asset — providing the foundation that data quality, AI initiatives, and regulatory compliance all depend on.

Core Components

Governance Structure

  • Data governance council & data owner/steward roles
  • Enterprise data policy & standards documentation
  • Data quality metrics & accountability

Policy Enforcement

  • Automated policy enforcement embedded in data platforms
  • Data access & usage approval workflows
  • Cross-border data transfer compliance

AI-Era Considerations

  • Training data provenance & lineage tracking
  • AI model data usage governance
  • Data governance alignment with privacy & security teams

Best Practices

  • Establish clear data ownership & stewardship accountability
  • Automate policy enforcement in data platforms rather than relying on manual review
  • Track data lineage & provenance, especially for AI training data
  • Align data governance closely with privacy, security & legal functions
  • Measure data quality with concrete, monitored metrics

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor the enterprise data governance program & council
  • Ensure data ownership & stewardship roles are clearly assigned
  • Fund automated policy enforcement tooling in data platforms
  • Align data governance with AI governance & privacy compliance programs
