AI Red Teaming

Adversarial testing of AI systems to surface safety, security, and misuse vulnerabilities

Overview

AI red teaming is adversarial testing specifically targeting AI and LLM systems — probing for prompt injection vulnerabilities, jailbreaks, harmful output generation, and data leakage through model outputs — distinct from traditional penetration testing which targets conventional application and network vulnerabilities. In 2026, AI red teaming has become a standard pre-deployment practice for organizations building or deploying customer-facing AI systems, often combining automated adversarial testing tools with structured human red team exercises.

Key Value: AI red teaming addresses a fundamentally different attack surface than traditional penetration testing — the vulnerabilities live in the model's behavior and training rather than in code, making specialized adversarial testing essential before deploying AI systems that handle sensitive tasks or public-facing interactions.

Core Components

Testing Categories

  • Prompt injection & jailbreak resistance testing
  • Harmful/biased output generation testing
  • Data leakage & training data extraction attempts

Methodology

  • Automated adversarial testing tools & frameworks
  • Structured human red team exercises
  • Continuous testing as models & prompts are updated

Program Integration

  • Pre-deployment gate for customer-facing AI systems
  • Findings feeding into prompt engineering & guardrail design
  • Coordination with vendor AI safety teams for third-party models

Best Practices

  • Treat AI red teaming as a required pre-deployment gate for customer-facing AI systems
  • Combine automated adversarial tools with structured human red team exercises
  • Re-test after any significant prompt, model, or guardrail change
  • Feed findings directly into prompt engineering & system guardrail design
  • Coordinate with third-party model vendors on their own safety testing practices

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor an AI red teaming program & pre-deployment gate policy
  • Ensure findings translate into concrete guardrail & prompt improvements
  • Fund both automated tooling & human red team expertise
  • Track AI red teaming coverage across all deployed AI systems
