CompTIA CySA+

An intermediate certification focused on security analytics, threat detection, and response

Overview

CompTIA CySA+ (Cybersecurity Analyst) is an intermediate-level, vendor-neutral certification focused specifically on security analytics — behavioral threat detection, vulnerability management, and incident response — positioned between foundational certifications (Security+) and advanced analyst credentials (GCIA, GCIH). In 2026, CySA+ remains a common certification path for SOC analysts and threat hunters building toward more specialized, vendor-specific security analyst certifications.

Key Value: CySA+ fills the gap between broad security fundamentals and deep specialist certifications, giving SOC analysts a structured validation of the specific analytical skills — log analysis, threat detection, vulnerability prioritization — that day-to-day security operations work actually requires.

Core Components

Exam Coverage

  • Security operations & threat detection methodology
  • Vulnerability management & prioritization
  • Incident response & reporting

Target Audience

  • SOC analysts (Tier 1/2) building analytical depth
  • Threat hunters & vulnerability management staff
  • Staff progressing from Security+ toward advanced analyst certifications

Certification Path

  • Builds on CompTIA Security+ foundational knowledge
  • Positioned before advanced analyst certifications (GCIA, GCIH)
  • Complements SOC-specific tooling certifications (SIEM vendors, etc.)

Best Practices

  • Pursue CySA+ for SOC analyst staff building toward more specialized analyst roles
  • Use it as a structured bridge between Security+ and advanced analyst certifications
  • Combine certification study with hands-on SIEM & detection tooling practice
  • Encourage vulnerability management staff to pursue it for structured prioritization skills
  • Plan continuing education as threat detection techniques and tooling evolve

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor CompTIA CySA+ for SOC analyst & threat hunting staff
  • Use it as a structured career progression step from Security+
  • Pair certification study with hands-on detection tooling experience
  • Budget for continuing education as the certification & threat landscape evolve
