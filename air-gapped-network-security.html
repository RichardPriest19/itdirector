Air-Gapped Network Security & Removable Media Control

Protecting isolated networks from the primary way they get compromised: removable media

Overview

Air-gapped networks — physically or logically isolated from the internet and corporate networks — are used to protect the most sensitive industrial, government, and research systems. Because they lack network connectivity, removable media (USB drives, external disks) is historically the primary route by which malware crosses the gap. In 2026, protecting these environments centers on strict removable-media control policies, dedicated media-scanning kiosks, device allowlisting, and monitoring for unauthorized media use at the boundary.

Key Value: Rigorous removable-media governance is the single highest-leverage control for air-gapped environments, since it closes the primary pathway attackers have historically used to bridge the physical/network isolation air gaps are designed to provide.

Core Components

Media Control Policy

  • USB device allowlisting & serial-number registration
  • Mandatory scanning kiosks before media crosses the gap
  • Write-once/read-only media requirements where feasible

Monitoring & Enforcement

  • Endpoint agent policies blocking unauthorized removable media
  • Audit logging of all media insertion/removal events
  • Physical security controls at air-gap boundary points

Personnel & Process

  • Formal media-transfer approval workflows
  • Staff training on air-gap hygiene & social engineering risks
  • Periodic audits of transfer logs & exceptions

Best Practices

  • Scan all removable media at a dedicated kiosk before it enters the air-gapped environment
  • Allowlist specific, registered devices; block unknown media by default
  • Log every media transfer event with an auditable chain of custody
  • Regularly audit exceptions & approval workflows for policy drift
  • Train personnel on the specific risks of social-engineered media introduction

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Define removable-media policy for air-gapped & isolated environments
  • Deploy scanning kiosks & device allowlisting controls
  • Ensure audit logging & periodic review of media transfer events
  • Oversee personnel training on air-gap security hygiene
