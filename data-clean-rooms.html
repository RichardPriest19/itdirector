Data Clean Rooms

Privacy-preserving environments enabling multiple parties to analyze shared data without exposing it

Overview

Data clean rooms are secure, privacy-preserving environments that let two or more parties jointly analyze combined datasets — for purposes like advertising measurement or partnership analytics — without either party gaining direct access to the other's underlying raw data. In 2026, clean rooms increasingly use confidential computing and differential privacy techniques to strengthen guarantees beyond earlier, purely contractual/access-control-based implementations, and are expanding beyond advertising into healthcare research and cross-industry data partnerships.

Key Value: Data clean rooms solve the specific problem of enabling valuable joint analysis between parties who have legitimate business reasons to collaborate but cannot or should not share raw underlying data directly — a distinct pattern from the broader Data Mesh approach to internal data ownership.

Core Components

Technical Approaches

  • Confidential computing-based clean rooms (hardware-enforced isolation)
  • Differential privacy for aggregate query results
  • Contractual/access-control-based clean rooms (lighter-weight)

Common Use Cases

  • Advertising measurement & attribution analysis
  • Healthcare research collaboration across institutions
  • Cross-industry partnership analytics (retail, financial services)

Governance

  • Query auditing & output disclosure controls
  • Data usage agreements defining permitted analysis
  • Regular review of privacy guarantee adequacy

Best Practices

  • Match the clean room technical approach to the actual sensitivity of data involved
  • Use confidential computing for the strongest technical privacy guarantees
  • Define clear data usage agreements before any joint analysis begins
  • Audit queries & outputs to prevent re-identification through aggregation
  • Reassess clean room privacy guarantees periodically as techniques and threats evolve

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Evaluate data clean room options for cross-party analytics partnerships
  • Ensure technical privacy guarantees match data sensitivity requirements
  • Establish clear governance & data usage agreements for each clean room use case
  • Coordinate with legal & privacy teams on clean room partnership terms
