Data Security Posture Management (DSPM)

Discovering where sensitive data actually lives and how exposed it is across cloud and SaaS environments

Overview

Data Security Posture Management (DSPM) discovers where sensitive data actually resides across cloud storage, databases, and SaaS applications, then assesses its exposure — who and what can access it, whether it's encrypted, whether it's been copied to unmanaged locations — independent of where IT assumed the data lived. In 2026, DSPM has become essential precisely because data proliferates faster than governance can track it manually, especially as AI pipelines copy and derive data into new locations at speed.

Key Value: DSPM answers the question traditional data classification programs often can't — not just how data *should* be classified and handled, but where sensitive data has actually ended up and how exposed it currently is, closing the gap between governance policy and real-world data sprawl.

Core Components

Discovery

  • Automated sensitive data discovery across cloud & SaaS
  • Shadow data identification (unmanaged copies, forgotten backups)
  • Data flow mapping to AI/ML pipelines & third parties

Risk Assessment

  • Access exposure analysis (who/what can reach sensitive data)
  • Encryption & control gap identification
  • Risk scoring by data sensitivity & exposure combined

Governance Integration

  • Alignment with data classification & governance programs
  • DLP & access control policy feedback loop
  • Compliance reporting for regulatory data protection requirements

Best Practices

  • Run automated discovery continuously, since data sprawl outpaces manual tracking
  • Prioritize remediation by the combination of data sensitivity and actual exposure
  • Extend discovery scope to cover AI/ML pipeline data flows specifically
  • Feed DSPM findings back into access control & DLP policy tuning
  • Treat DSPM as complementary to, not a replacement for, data classification governance

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Deploy DSPM tooling to discover sensitive data exposure across the estate
  • Ensure findings drive concrete access control & encryption remediation
  • Extend DSPM coverage to AI/ML data pipelines specifically
  • Use DSPM data to strengthen data governance & classification programs
