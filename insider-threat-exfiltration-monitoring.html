Insider Threat & Data Exfiltration Monitoring

Detecting unauthorized data movement, whether by external attackers or trusted insiders

Overview

Insider threat and data exfiltration monitoring focuses on detecting unauthorized movement of sensitive data — whether by a malicious or negligent insider, a compromised account, or an external attacker operating with stolen credentials. In 2026, enterprise programs combine User & Entity Behavior Analytics (UEBA), data loss prevention (DLP) integration, and privileged-access monitoring to flag anomalous data access and movement patterns before large-scale exfiltration completes.

Key Value: Behavior-based monitoring catches slow, covert data exfiltration — the kind designed to stay under the radar of simple volume-based alerts — by focusing on what's unusual for a given user or system, not just how much data moved.

Core Components

Behavioral Detection

  • User & Entity Behavior Analytics (UEBA)
  • Anomalous access-pattern & privilege-use detection
  • Peer-group baselining for unusual data access

Data Movement Controls

  • Data Loss Prevention (DLP) integration across endpoints, email & cloud
  • Egress monitoring for unusual destinations or volumes
  • Removable media & cloud-upload restriction policies

Program Governance

  • Insider risk program with HR & legal coordination
  • Least-privilege access review cadence
  • Offboarding & access-revocation workflows

Best Practices

  • Deploy UEBA to baseline normal behavior and flag deviations
  • Integrate DLP across endpoint, email, and cloud storage channels
  • Enforce least-privilege access with regular entitlement reviews
  • Automate access revocation immediately upon role change or offboarding
  • Coordinate insider risk investigations with HR & legal from the outset

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor a formal insider risk program with clear governance
  • Ensure UEBA & DLP coverage across critical data repositories
  • Maintain least-privilege access & regular entitlement reviews
  • Coordinate cross-functionally on investigations & response
