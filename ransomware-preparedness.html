Ransomware Preparedness & Response

A dedicated playbook for preventing, surviving, and recovering from a ransomware attack

Overview

Ransomware preparedness and response is a dedicated discipline within broader cyber resilience, focused specifically on the unique dynamics of ransomware incidents — rapid encryption spread, extortion negotiation pressure, and the decision of whether to pay. In 2026, mature programs emphasize immutable, tested backups as the primary recovery lever, pre-established relationships with ransomware negotiation and legal specialists, and rehearsed decision trees for the pay/don't-pay question well before an actual incident forces it under pressure.

Key Value: Ransomware-specific preparation — rather than relying on generic incident response plans — matters because ransomware's speed of spread and extortion dynamics create decision pressure (pay or not, how fast can we restore) that generic IR playbooks don't address in enough operational detail.

Core Components

Prevention

  • Segmentation limiting ransomware spread blast radius
  • Email & endpoint controls targeting common initial access vectors
  • Privileged access restrictions limiting mass encryption capability

Recovery Readiness

  • Immutable, air-gapped, regularly tested backups
  • Recovery time validation through regular restore drills
  • Alternate/out-of-band communication plan if primary systems are encrypted

Incident Decision-Making

  • Pre-established ransomware negotiation & legal counsel relationships
  • Rehearsed pay/don't-pay decision framework & authority chain
  • Law enforcement & regulatory notification procedures

Best Practices

  • Test backup restoration regularly under realistic time pressure, not just backup completion
  • Maintain immutable, air-gapped backups isolated from the production network
  • Establish negotiation & legal counsel relationships before an incident, not during one
  • Rehearse the pay/don't-pay decision framework with executive leadership in advance
  • Maintain an out-of-band communication plan for when primary systems are encrypted

IT Director Responsibilities

  • Sponsor ransomware-specific preparedness planning & tabletop exercises
  • Ensure immutable backup capability is tested against realistic recovery time objectives
  • Establish pre-incident relationships with negotiation, legal & law enforcement contacts
  • Own the executive decision framework for ransomware extortion scenarios
